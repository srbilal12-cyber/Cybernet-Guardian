04 / MICRO-LESSON

What does your network fingerprint tell you?

A fingerprint is a compact description of the environment, not a person's identity.

Subnet
Defines the local IPv4 range your device considers directly reachable.
Gateway
Usually your router — the next hop used to reach other networks.
ARP / MAC
ARP maps local IPv4 addresses to link-layer addresses when the operating system has learned them.
Open TCP port
A service is listening or accepting connections on that port. Identify the service and check whether you need it.
Permission & accuracy: Use only your own network or one you are authorized to assess. Some devices block discovery. Port checks are limited and are not a vulnerability assessment. This app does not sniff Wi-Fi traffic, identify people, or detect room presence.
